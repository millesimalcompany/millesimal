How it starts
01 · Diagnostic

20 meetings you have already booked

Written notes only. Within a week, a private report shows which criteria your meetings would fail and where your notes cost you disputes. Free for the first two agencies.

02 · Pilot · 60 days

A receipt for every meeting you book

One line in your calendar invite. The prospect confirms the meeting to us. Your client gets a free certificate, and contested meetings are ruled on at no extra charge.

03 · Rating

A number you decide when to share

A private rating statement at day 60. Your contracting clients can ask to see it. Anyone else needs your written consent. Certification follows once there is enough to audit.

Why a rating

Buyers trust a rating more than a case study.

01

A case study is your pick

Every agency has a few good logos. Buyers know you chose them, so they give them little weight.

02

A rating is someone else’s pick

We pick 20 meetings from at least two campaigns and months, judged against a definition published in advance.

03

It moves the talk away from price

When quality cannot be measured, buyers compare on price. A score gives a careful agency something to charge for.

Expect your rating to be below your show rate. Attendance is only one of seven criteria.
Certification

How certification works.

An audited share of your submitted meetings that met the Standard over a set period. You may not present it as an endorsement, recommendation or guarantee.

Before you sign

What certification costs you.

Be sure of these four things before you sign, not at renewal.

01
Your first number may be lower than you expect.Seven pass or fail checks with no partial credit are stricter than any internal QA.
02
Your past rating stays after you leave.Withdrawing does not erase it. It stays, marked as historic. That is what gives a current rating value.
03
Falsified evidence is published.The one exception to publishing only with consent. Without it, the scheme would mean nothing.
04
You will lose some rulings.One of our three published specimens rules against the agency on a single checkable fact.
Free to do now

Three things to fix this week

Record the ICP when the campaign starts

Dated and in writing, with the employee band and any timing condition stated so they can be checked. If it is too loose to rule against, do not launch.

Ask for the suppression list

Without a list, a buyer cannot object under criterion 7. Asking is free and removes your most common dispute.

Name the vendor in the invitation

Criterion 5 depends on what the attendee accepted. Name the vendor and say the meeting is commercial, and the invitation will hold up.
